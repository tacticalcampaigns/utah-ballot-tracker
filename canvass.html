Sources & method. Each county’s Standardized Canvass Statistics Report as posted to the state reports page (General11032026), parsed from the county’s .xlsx filing except Wayne County, transcribed from its scanned canvass packet. Vote-history figures are VISTA data as reported by each county on its canvass form. “Derived total received” = ballots counted + total rejected; “total rejected” = curable ballots not cured + non-curable rejections + provisionals not counted. sums across the 28 parsed counties only; excludes Cache County (posted but unparseable) and all unposted counties. Canvass figures are the authoritative final numbers for canvassed counties.
Sources: Utah Lieutenant Governor’s election results (electionresults.utah.gov) — state All-Results reports and county Ballot Statistics reports; county clerk reconciliation pages; the State Dashboard (November 2026) for registration. District boundaries: Utah Geospatial Resource Center (AGRC). All figures are unofficial until canvassed. This tracker is not affiliated with the State of Utah or any county.
