Est. left = county’s estimated total ballots received − ballots in results. Each county’s estimate comes from that county’s own Ballot Statistics report posted on the state results site (26 of 29 counties, read Nov 6; the as-of time under each figure is the county report’s own timestamp). Hard county numbers are marked *; the 3 counties without a usable report (Daggett, Morgan, San Juan) are estimated at the median yet-to-report ratio of the hard counties still counting (1.33% of reported) and marked ~. 6 counties (Cache, Emery, Garfield, Kane, Millard, Piute) have completed their official canvass — their counts are final and their yet-to-report is 0; the received-minus-counted remainder in each is rejected ballots (rejected provisionals, uncured curable ballots, and non-curable rejections), not outstanding votes. “Registered” is active registered voters as of Nov 3 (state voted file). Ballots in results = max ballots-cast across contests in the state report. County reports are snapshots (some from Nov 3–4), so a stale estimate can understate what’s left if more mail arrived since. There is no late mail in Utah: total ballots received is fixed at the close of polls.
