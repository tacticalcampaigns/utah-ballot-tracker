Each bar stacks only votes cast in that race, as a share of all ballots in the filter — so a shorter bar means more undervotes. Segment labels are the share of votes cast in that race (undervotes and overvotes excluded from the denominator); hover a segment for the raw ballot count and that race’s undervotes. The number above each bar is the ballots it covers: for SD, only ballots with an SD race on the ballot (SD seats are staggered — only 15 were on the 2026 ballot). HD and Amendment B totals likewise exclude federal-only ballots, which carry no HD race and no Amendment B value.
Sources & method. Cast-vote records published by the counties (Disclosure Controlled CVR files) via the state results site; 792,339 ballot rows from 20 counties. “Other” in the CD-choice filter is third-party and independent CD votes; ballots with no vote in the CD race (undervotes) appear only under “All”. Registration and official turnout are the tracker’s figures (active registered voters, Nov 3 voted file; turnout = ballots counted ÷ active registered). All figures are unofficial until canvassed. Source CVR master: utah-statewide-cvr-2026.csv (792,339 ballots, 20 counties merged). Candidate->party mapping from All-Results_2026-11-17_1407-MST.xlsx (Summary Results sheet, authoritative); 1,945,452 candidate cells mapped via Excel, 0 via CVR party-prefix fallback, 0 unmapped. Registration denominator: active registered voters, Ali Majid Tableau extract Nov 2026 (Nov 3 voted file); statewide 1,784,255 = sum of county Total registered (Nov 3 voted file). Turnout = ballots in results / registered, the tracker's definition (build_site.py). Per-district turnout ballots = 'Ballots Cast' for that office in the Summary Results sheet of the same All Results file; district-stats.json does not carry ballots counted per district (only candidate vote totals). Amendment B values in the CVR are FOR/AGAINST (family A, Weber title-case, Rich with candidate-ID suffixes), mapped to Yes/No; YES/NO would be accepted too. AmB 'total' = ballots with a non-blank AmB value; 406 ballots have a blank AmB (federal-only ballots, see below) and are excluded from the AmB total so Yes+No+undervotes+overvotes reconciles to total. 406 ballots have a CD race but no HD race, no SD race, and blank Amendments A/B (federal-only ballots: a CD vote and nothing else on the ballot). They count in 'All' groups and in HD/SD 'no_race', and in no HD/SD geo. CD-party groups: R/D/Other = ballots whose CD choice was that party; CD undervotes/overvotes are in 'All' only. Write-ins count as Other and are also reported separately under 'writeins'. Overvotes count as neither a vote nor an undervote. Overvotes in tabulated races (CD/SD/HD/AmB): 1. Write-ins in tabulated races: 0 (file-wide write-ins in local contests are not counted here). Millard County is posted as a PDF, not XLSX, and is excluded from the merge (see SCHEMA.md §8); its tracker_counted is shown with cvr_ballots 0.
Sources: Utah Lieutenant Governor’s election results (electionresults.utah.gov) — state All-Results reports and county Ballot Statistics reports; county clerk reconciliation pages; Ali Majid’s November 2026 Dashboard (Tableau Public) for registration. District boundaries: Utah Geospatial Resource Center (AGRC). All figures are unofficial until canvassed. This tracker is not affiliated with the State of Utah or any county.
