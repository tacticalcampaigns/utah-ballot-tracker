Sources & method. Cast-vote records published by the counties (Disclosure Controlled CVR files) via the state results site; 991,225 ballot rows from 22 counties. “Other” in the partisan vote choice filter is third-party and independent votes in the chosen contest; ballots with no vote in that contest (undervotes) appear only under “All”. Registration and official turnout are the tracker’s figures (active registered voters, Nov 3 voted file; turnout = ballots counted ÷ active registered). County registration and turnout use total registered voters (Nov 2026). All figures are unofficial until canvassed. Source CVR master: utah-statewide-cvr-2026.csv (991,225 ballots, 22 counties merged). Candidate->party mapping from All-Results_2026-11-17_1407-MST.xlsx (Summary Results sheet, authoritative); 2,413,262 candidate cells mapped via Excel, 0 via CVR party-prefix fallback, 0 unmapped. Registration denominator: active registered voters (Nov 2026) from the State Dashboard; statewide 1,784,255 = sum of county Total registered (Nov 3 voted file). Turnout = ballots in results / registered, the tracker's definition. County registration and turnout use TOTAL registered voters (Nov 2026), not active. Per-district turnout ballots = 'Ballots Cast' for that office in the Summary Results sheet of the same All Results file; district-stats.json does not carry ballots counted per district (only candidate vote totals). Amendment B values in the CVR are FOR/AGAINST (family A, Weber title-case, Rich with candidate-ID suffixes), mapped to Yes/No; YES/NO would be accepted too. AmB 'total' = ballots with a non-blank AmB value; 717 ballots have a blank AmB (federal-only ballots, see below) and are excluded from the AmB total so Yes+No+undervotes+overvotes reconciles to total. 717 ballots have a CD contest but no HD contest, no SD contest, and blank Amendments A/B (federal-only ballots: a CD vote and nothing else on the ballot). They count in 'All' groups, in no HD/SD geography, and in the HD/SD no-contest counts. Partisan vote choice groups: R/D/Other = ballots whose choice in the defining contest (U.S. House, State Senate, or State House) was that party; undervotes and overvotes in the defining contest, and ballots with no such contest on the ballot, are in 'All' only. Write-ins count as Other and are also reported separately under 'writeins'. Overvotes count as neither a vote nor an undervote. Overvotes in tabulated contests (CD/SD/HD/AmB): 1. Write-ins in tabulated contests: 1902 (file-wide write-ins in local contests are not counted here). Millard County's CVR was posted as a PDF (an Excel print-to-PDF, 1,045 content pages in 11 column blocks); it was reconstructed by coordinate extraction and validated contest-by-contest against the official Millard results before merging.
Sources: Utah Lieutenant Governor’s election results (electionresults.utah.gov) — state All-Results reports and county Ballot Statistics reports; county clerk reconciliation pages; the State Dashboard (November 2026) for registration. District boundaries: Utah Geospatial Resource Center (AGRC). All figures are unofficial until canvassed. This tracker is not affiliated with the State of Utah or any county.
