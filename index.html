Voter credit = real VISTA vote histories for the 28 counties with parsed canvass statistics (1,016,350) + estimated total ballots received for the other 1 counties (42,942). Counties credit voters as ballots are signature-verified, so credited voters track ballots received, not ballots counted. As counties canvass, real histories replace the estimates; the figure is fully real once the canvass completes Nov 23. Cache County has canvassed but its statistics file is unreadable, so its known 42,942 received is used as the estimate.
Est. left — how it’s figured (yet to be reported = county’s estimated total received − ballots in state results): Each county’s estimated total received comes from that county’s own Ballot Statistics report posted on the state results site ( 28 of 29 counties — see the Counties page for each county’s figure and as-of time; the reports are re-checked hourly and the estimates update when a county posts a new report). Hard county numbers are marked *: Salt Lake County 397,413 est. received − 390,832 reported = 0 yet to report (county report as of Nov 17, 5:34pm (canvass)); Utah County 197,667 est. received − 194,737 reported = 0 yet to report (county report as of Nov 17, 2:16pm (canvass)). 28 counties (Beaver, Box Elder, Cache, Carbon, Daggett, Davis, Duchesne, Emery, Garfield, Grand, Iron, Juab, Kane, Millard, Morgan, Piute, Rich, Salt Lake, Sanpete, Sevier, Summit, Tooele, Uintah, Utah, Wasatch, Washington, Wayne, Weber) have completed their official canvass — their counts are final and their yet-to-report is 0; the received-minus-counted remainder in each is rejected ballots (rejected provisionals, uncured curable ballots, and non-curable rejections), not outstanding votes. The 1 counties without a usable report (San Juan) are estimated at the median yet-to-report ratio of the hard counties still counting (3.62% of reported) — together they contribute only ~186 to the statewide total. Note the county reports are snapshots (some from Nov 3–4), so a stale estimate can understate what’s left if more mail arrived since — and the “yet to report” pool includes provisional and curable ballots, some of which will not ultimately count. For reference, the already-in-hand backlogs are smaller: Utah County verified-but-not-counted 0, Salt Lake County in-process 5,578 — the gap between those and the “yet to report” figures is ballots the counties estimate are still arriving by mail. There is no late mail in Utah: total ballots received is fixed at the close of polls.
Likely to count (est.) — the yet-to-report pool minus ballots the county has already counted but the state results file hasn’t published yet (the county Summary Results PDF “Ballots Cast” header runs ahead of the contest feed) and minus ballots expected to be rejected (the county’s own rejected figures where available — Salt Lake County 5,502, Washington County 1,939; otherwise the canvass-derived rejection rate of 1.67% across the 28 parsed canvass counties), floored at 0. Canvassed counties are 0. The point figure has no late-cure allowance — a county at 0 may still see a few late cures before the Nov 23 canvass. Contest projections use the likely-to-count pool; the raw yet-to-report figure stays visible alongside. See the Counties page for each county’s inputs.
Definitions: the statewide 2,099,221 is total registered voters (State Dashboard, November 2026); county “Registered” figures on the Counties page are active registered voters (Nov 3 voted file), so statewide turnout % is not directly comparable to county turnout %. “Ballots in results” = max ballots-cast across contests per county (state report, Precinct Results sheet). The % beside it is its share of estimated total ballots received statewide. All figures are unofficial until canvassed.
