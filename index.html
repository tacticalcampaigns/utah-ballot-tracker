Each bar shows the U.S. House-race votes reported on that calendar day (MST), split by the candidate’s party. Utah does not report the party of ballots cast, so the split is by candidate party: Republican, Democratic, or Other (Libertarian, Unaffiliated, and all other parties). CD votes are slightly fewer than ballots counted — ballots with no vote in the CD race (undervotes) and overvotes are not in any candidate’s total. The Nov 3 bar is Utah County’s election-night results only (the first county results captured, 11:19pm); the first statewide results came at 12:23am Nov 4. Votes are assigned to the day of the release that first reported them. Total CD candidate votes to date: 1,029,493.
Voter credit = estimated total ballots received statewide (county Ballot Statistics reports + modeled counties); counties credit voters as ballots are signature-verified, so credited voters track ballots received, not ballots counted. No statewide vote-history (VISTA) total is published in the pipeline’s sources; this figure is the estimate, not a VISTA count.
Est. left — how it’s figured (yet to be reported = county’s estimated total received − ballots in state results): Each county’s estimated total received comes from that county’s own Ballot Statistics report posted on the state results site (26 of 29 counties — see the Counties page for each county’s figure and as-of time; the reports are re-checked hourly and the estimates update when a county posts a new report). Hard county numbers are marked *: Salt Lake County 396,269 est. received − 375,666 reported = 20,603 yet to report (county report as of 2026-11-12); Utah County 197,657 est. received − 191,161 reported = 6,496 yet to report (county report as of Nov 10, 9:34pm). 6 counties (Cache, Emery, Garfield, Kane, Millard, Piute) have completed their official canvass — their counts are final and their yet-to-report is 0; the received-minus-counted remainder in each is rejected ballots (rejected provisionals, uncured curable ballots, and non-curable rejections), not outstanding votes. The 3 counties without a usable report (Daggett, Morgan, San Juan) are estimated at the median yet-to-report ratio of the hard counties still counting (1.33% of reported) — together they contribute only ~132 to the statewide total. Note the county reports are snapshots (some from Nov 3–4), so a stale estimate can understate what’s left if more mail arrived since — and the “yet to report” pool includes provisional and curable ballots, some of which will not ultimately count. For reference, the already-in-hand backlogs are smaller: Utah County verified-but-not-counted 2,180, Salt Lake County in-process 15,848 — the gap between those and the “yet to report” figures is ballots the counties estimate are still arriving by mail. There is no late mail in Utah: total ballots received is fixed at the close of polls.
Definitions: the statewide 2,099,221 is total registered voters (Ali Majid’s November 2026 Dashboard, Tableau Public); county “Registered” figures on the Counties page are active registered voters (Nov 3 voted file), so statewide turnout % is not directly comparable to county turnout %. “Ballots in results” = max ballots-cast across contests per county (state report, Precinct Results sheet). The % beside it is its share of estimated total ballots received statewide. All figures are unofficial until canvassed.
