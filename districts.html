How to read this: “Registered” is active registered voters from the Tableau voter-file extract (Ali Majid’s November 2026 Dashboard, published Nov 2, 2026); the R / D / Unaffiliated / Other columns show the count and the share of the district’s registered voters. “R votes 2026” / “D votes 2026” are the votes for Republican and Democratic candidates in the 2026 general election, with each party’s share of all votes cast in the race in parentheses — votes for other parties (Forward, Libertarian, Unaffiliated, United Utah, Constitution) are counted in the total. “Change 2026 vs 2024 (pts)” is the 2026 GOP share of total votes minus the 2024 baseline, in percentage points — green with a + when the GOP share rose, red with a − when it fell — shown only for actually contested races (a dash when there is no R candidate, when Democrats take 100%, or when the R candidate takes 100%). Senate seats not on the 2026 ballot show “not up in 2026”; uncontested races show the single candidate. All figures are unofficial until canvassed. GOP vote strength 2024 is, in effect, the generic Republican ballot share in 2024: (Republican votes for the four state constitutional offices in the 2024 general election — Governor, Attorney General, Treasurer, Auditor — divided by 4) ÷ total 2024 presidential votes in the district, computed from the official state 2024 results at precinct level. The Utah GOP precinct file is used only to place each precinct in its current district; its own vote values are not used. Statewide the figure is 57.8%. Coverage: all 108 districts have a value. About 0.9% of precinct Republican votes could not be assigned to a district (mostly unincorporated Utah County precincts the crosswalk file has no row for) and 0.07% of votes were counted at county level with no precinct attached, so district figures are understated by a fraction of a point at most.
Sources: Utah Lieutenant Governor’s election results (electionresults.utah.gov) — state All-Results reports and county Ballot Statistics reports; county clerk reconciliation pages; Ali Majid’s November 2026 Dashboard (Tableau Public) for registration.
